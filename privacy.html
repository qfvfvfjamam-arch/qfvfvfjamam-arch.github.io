Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1.Summary

denycoch runs entirely on the owner's own Windows computer. It has no servers and no user accounts of its own. It does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.

2.Data from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, denycoch uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with these permissions:

PermissionWhy it is used
user.info.basicShow which account is connected (display name and avatar).
video.upload, video.publishUpload and post the videos you select to your own account.

The access token TikTok issues is stored only in a file on your computer. It is sent only to TikTok's own API.

3.Your videos

Recordings and the clips made from them stay on your computer. A video leaves the computer only when you choose to post it, and then it goes directly to TikTok.

4.What is not collected

  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising identifiers.
  • No contacts, messages, location or payment data.
  • No data about other TikTok users.

5.Keeping and deleting data

The token is kept until you disconnect. To delete everything, remove denycoch in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions, then delete the app folder from your computer.

6.Children

denycoch is not intended for anyone under 13, or the minimum age for TikTok in their country.
