Desktop app · Windows

denycoch

Turns long Counter-Strike 2 stream recordings into vertical highlight clips and posts the ones you pick to your own TikTok.

  • 1080 × 1920 clips
  • Kill and speech detection
  • Runs on your PC
  • No accounts, no tracking
HE'S RIGHT THERE!

How it works

  1. Open a recording

    Any MP4 of your CS2 stream with the webcam in a corner.

  2. Find the moments

    Kills are read from the in-game HUD, speech from the audio with timestamps.

  3. Review the clips

    Close events are merged into one scene. You tick the clips to keep.

  4. Export and post

    Webcam on top, gameplay below, subtitles on. Posting to TikTok happens only when you choose.

denycoch is a personal tool. It connects to TikTok through the official Login Kit and Content Posting API and uploads only to the account you connect. It is not made or endorsed by TikTok.
